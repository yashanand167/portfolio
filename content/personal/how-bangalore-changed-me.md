When people ask me where I'm from, the answer is simple.

**Kathmandu, Nepal.**

But if they ask me where I became myself, the answer is different.

**Bangalore.**

When I left Kathmandu on **September 20, 2022**, I thought I was only moving cities.

I didn't realize I was leaving behind an older version of myself.

## Before Bangalore

My world wasn't small because of where I lived.

It was small because of my comfort zone.

Crossing a busy road alone scared me.

Booking a cab felt intimidating.

Talking to strangers wasn't something I was comfortable with.

I depended on people for almost everything.

Looking back, those things feel ordinary now. But confidence isn't something you're born with.

It's built through experience.

And Bangalore gave me plenty of it.

## Closing a Chapter

High school was one of the hardest periods of my life.

It slowly chipped away at my confidence and made me question myself more than I should have.

By the time it ended, I made one decision that changed everything.

I walked away from people who no longer belonged in my future.

Not out of anger.

Not out of revenge.

Simply because I realized some relationships only keep you attached to a version of yourself you're trying to outgrow.

I've never regretted that decision.

Sometimes growth begins with letting go.

## A New Beginning

I came to Bangalore with one plan.

Finish engineering.

Graduate.

Go back home.

Instead, Bangalore became home.

In almost four years, I only visited my family twice. It wasn't easy, but every time I returned, this city felt familiar in a way I never expected.

More importantly, it changed how I think.

Here, ambition feels normal.

Walk into a café and you'll find people building startups, designing products, writing code, or chasing ideas they genuinely believe in.

That mindset is contagious.

I arrived believing success meant getting a degree and a good job.

Today, success means building meaningful products, learning continuously, and creating work that outlives me.

Somewhere between college, side projects, countless late nights, and too many cups of coffee, I found the path I actually wanted to follow.

## What Bangalore Taught Me

This city taught me that comfort zones quietly limit your potential.

It taught me that failure isn't something to fear—it's proof you were willing to try.

It taught me that not every relationship is meant to last.

And most importantly, it taught me that the people around you shape the size of your ambitions.

## What's Ahead

This city gave me something I didn't know I was looking for.

Direction.

The next ten years are about honoring that.

I want to build relentlessly, keep learning, explore the world, and become the best version of myself. To create products I'm proud of, solve meaningful problems, and leave behind work that matters.

That means making a few sacrifices.

No **relationships**.

No chasing comfort.

No settling for average.

Just discipline, curiosity, and consistent effort.

It won't always be easy.

There will be failures, doubts, and moments where giving up feels easier than moving forward.

But I've made that commitment to myself.

Ten years of focused work.

Ten years of becoming someone my younger self would be proud of.

When I look back a decade from now, I don't want to wonder *"What if?"*

I want to know that I gave everything I had.
